Cookies and measurement

This site measures its audience with Matomo, hosted by the publisher at mamoto.rslt.fr. Each page asks the reader's browser to load matomo.js and a Matomo Tag Manager container from there, and to report the visit and the events the container is set to record, which discloses the reader's IP address, the page, the referrer and the browser's details to that server. The figures serve only to know which pages are read; they are not sold or passed to anyone else.

Matomo sets these first-party cookies:

  • _pk_id: a random visitor identifier, so a return visit is counted once; kept 13 months.
  • _pk_ses: that a visit is under way; kept 30 minutes.
  • _pk_ref: the site the visit came from; kept 6 months.

A reader can refuse or delete them in the browser's settings, or block mamoto.rslt.fr; the site works the same without them.

Data seen while serving a page

Serving a page involves the reader's IP address. GitHub, as host, records technical data of that kind; what it does with it is set out in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

These pages also load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so a reader's browser requests them from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com and discloses its IP address to Google in doing so. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
